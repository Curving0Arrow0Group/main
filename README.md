# Curving Arrow Group 

## About Me

At Curving Arrow Group, we pioneer data-driven investment strategies at the intersection of technology and market intelligence. As Chairman and CEO, I guide our team in transforming complex market insights into actionable investment opportunities, drawing from my extensive background in public policy implementation and technology development.

My journey includes spearheading technology commercialization initiatives as a Translational Research Fellow at Memorial University of Newfoundland, where I developed strategic partnerships with industry leaders and conducted comprehensive market analyses for emerging technologies. This experience, combined with my years of managing multi-billion USD public sector portfolios and implementing national-scale energy policies, enables our team to identify unique market opportunities with precision and foresight.

We blend rigorous academic research with practical market intelligence, exemplified by our recent publications and investment strategies. Our approach is strengthened by my hands-on experience in managing large-scale infrastructure projects and fostering international collaborations across Asia and North America.
